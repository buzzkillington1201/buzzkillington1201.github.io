Porch

A private home app for one household.

Porch is a personal, non-commercial app used by a single family in their own home. It shows the household's own Google Nest thermostat, doorbell and cameras alongside the family's TVs and home network, from a server that runs inside the home.

It is not offered to the public, has no accounts to sign up for, and is used only by the members of that household with their own Google account.

How Porch handles Google account data is described in the privacy policy.
