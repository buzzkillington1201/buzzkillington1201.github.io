Privacy policy

Porch · last updated 25 October 2026

Porch is a private app used by one household in their own home (see the home page). This page explains what it does with Google account data.

What Porch accesses

With the account owner's permission, Porch uses Google's Smart Device Management API to read and control the household's own Nest devices: thermostat readings and settings, and doorbell and camera events and live video. It requests nothing else from the Google account: no email, contacts, files or profile details beyond what Google's sign-in requires.

Where the data goes

The data is used only to show and control those devices for the household. It is processed and stored on a server inside the household's home. It is not sold, not shared with anyone, not used for advertising, and not sent to any other service, except Google itself when Porch asks Google to change a device.

Keeping and deleting it

Device readings and recordings stay on the home server and are kept only as long as the household wants them. Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's third-party access page or the Nest partner connections page; Porch then can no longer reach the devices.

Use of Google data

Porch's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
